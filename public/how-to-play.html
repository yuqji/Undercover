How to play Undercover

Undercover — also known as Who Is the Undercover, Who's the Spy word game, or 谁是卧底 — is a fast word party game of clues, bluffing and voting. Everyone gets the same secret word… except the Undercover, who gets a slightly different one and doesn't know it.

No download, no sign-up. Works on phones. 3–16 players.

The roles

  • Civilians — the majority. They all share the same word (for example coffee).
  • Undercover — gets a close word (for example tea). Nobody, not even the Undercover, is told who has the different word.
  • Mr. White (optional) — gets no word at all and has to bluff from the other clues.

How a round works

  1. Get your word. Keep it secret.
  2. Give a clue. In turn, each player types one short clue (up to 20 characters) that describes their word without saying it. Too obvious and the Undercover learns your word; too vague and people suspect you.
  3. Vote. Everyone votes for the player who seems different. The player with the most votes is out and their role is revealed. A tie gets one re-vote; tie again and nobody leaves.
  4. Repeat until one side wins.

How to win

  • Civilians win when every Undercover (and Mr. White) is voted out.
  • The Undercover wins by surviving until they're as many as the Civilians.
  • Mr. White wins by surviving to the end — or, if caught, by guessing the Civilians' word.

Tips

  • As a Civilian, pick clues only someone with your word would get — but not so specific that you hand it to the Undercover.
  • If the other clues don't quite fit your word, you might be the Undercover. Blend in and stay vague.
  • Watch the order: the first speaker has the least to go on, so their clue is the hardest to judge.

Playing online with friends

On Undercover you can create a private room and share the link, or jump into a public room with players from anywhere. Pick word packs (food, movies, animals and more), add Mr. White, chat during the game, add friends you played with, and optionally sign in with Google to keep your name, look and stats on every device. Great for parties, video calls, classrooms and Friday team fun at work.
