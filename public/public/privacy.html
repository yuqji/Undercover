Privacy Policy

Last updated November 22, 2026

Undercover is a free online word party game. This page explains what we keep and why.

Playing without an account

You can play without signing in. Your nickname, avatar and friends list are stored only in your own browser. Room chat and clues live in the server's memory while a game runs and are gone when the room closes.

If you sign in with Google

Sign-in is optional. If you use it, we receive your Google account ID and email address from Google (through our sign-in provider, Supabase). We store your nickname, avatar, game stats (games played and won), your friends list and your blocked list so they follow you across devices. We do not read your contacts, Gmail, Drive or anything else in your Google account.

Contact form

Messages you send through "Contact us" are emailed to the site owner so we can reply. They are not shown to other players.

Sharing

We don't sell your data or use it for advertising. Other players see only your nickname, avatar and public player ID. Data is stored with our service providers Supabase (accounts) and Render (hosting).

Deleting your data

Use "Contact us" in the game to ask us to delete your account and everything stored with it, and we will do so.
